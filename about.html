my take on creating with AI

I know there are a lot of people saying AI can't be art, and that you hadn't actually created anything yourself. I disagree. For me it is a tool same as Adobe software or a glue gun. And it all depends on the concept and context of your creation no matter the tool. I use it to bring my ideas to life, I had created things I never dreamed I would, like music videos and fashion shoots.

In my prompting I respect copy rights and never mention names of other artists or submit someone elses art as a reference for my own profit. Also we must remember this is still an evolving situation, you could say we are in the middle of the storm and we are not sure yet how it will settle down, in the meantime I am just experimenting with whatever tools I can put my hands on.

I did not abandon real handmade craft, I still illustrate and I will always gravitate towards the natural and the tactile. Even my first ever AI generated visual was an embroidery. That by the way took forever because I regenerated every tiny bit until it was perfect, so who's to say it is not a craft?
I believe, talent (and lack there of) is showing no matter what tools we use.

a peek into my techniques

The main tool I use for Image generation is Midjourney, I create a style profile for every project to steer the model in the right direction. Complementary to that I use whatever other models I need for specific tasks. For video I chose one of the latest models - whichever one is best for the task at hand. I create flows in Figma Weave for larger scaled projects. I also create music in Suno and Narrations in Eleven labs, and if needed I create interfaces to generate certain kinds of visuals at scale using Claude Code or Codex. The latter also serve me in building websites like this one. Hoping to dive deeper into creating and working on 3D with AI
